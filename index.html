Marcus Heppner's Favorite Movies

GoodFellas (1990)

Directed by Martin Scorsese. 93% on Rotten Tomatoes. 8.7/10 on iMDB. 4.5/5 on Letterboxd.

Fun Fact: Edward McDonald, the actual U.S. attorney who placed the real Henry Hill into the witness protection program, played himself in the final interrogation scene.

Why do I like GoodFellas

  • Quotable narration from Ray Liotta, as well as standout performances from Robert De Niro and Joe Pesci.
  • Gritty, unfiltered view into the life of a ganster, while stil remaining entertaining.
  • Memeroble soundtrack that leans on music from the correlated decades of the story, with my favorite being "Layla" by Derek and the Dominoes.

A page about our home planet

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only place we know of with life. From space it looks mostly blue, because water covers about 71% of its surface.

Earth is about 4.5 billion years old. It spins once a day, circles the Sun once a year, and carries about 8 billion people along for the ride.

Three facts about Earth

  • One trip around the Sun takes 365.25 days. That extra quarter day is why we have leap years.
  • The deepest known point in the ocean is about 11 kilometers down.
  • The Moon drifts away from Earth about 4 centimeters every year.
